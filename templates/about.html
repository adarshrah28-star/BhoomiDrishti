What this platform does

BhoomiDrishti is a decision-support prototype for the period before land is acquired and before construction starts. It brings acquisition status, terrain, hazard exposure and preliminary geotechnical indicators into one view, then produces a delay probability, a site suitability score, the factors driving them, and an action list.

The point is not to replace engineering judgement. It is to tell a planner which of forty projects needs attention this month, and which of three candidate sites deserves the survey budget.

What it does not do

  • It cannot determine pile depth, foundation depth, bearing capacity, reinforcement or structural design
  • It does not forecast that a landslide or flood will occur on a given date
  • It does not claim real-world accuracy: the model is trained on synthetic data
  • It is not integrated with any government system, and displays no official seals or logos
  • It is not a substitute for statutory clearance, survey or certified investigation

How a prediction is explained

For each input, the prediction is re-run with only that field reset to a healthy baseline. The drop in predicted risk is that field's contribution for this specific project, and the contributions are normalised to percentages.

This is a form of ablation attribution. It is simpler than SHAP, it needs no extra dependency, and it is local rather than global, so two projects with the same risk score can have entirely different explanations.
