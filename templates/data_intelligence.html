How data would flow in production

1. Authorised access
Data-sharing agreements with the revenue department, executing agency and state disaster authority. No public scraping.
2. Scheduled ingestion
A nightly job pulls project status, acquisition progress and rainfall observations into a staging database.
3. Validation
Records are checked for missing khasra entries, duplicate parcels and out-of-range values before they reach the model.
4. Retraining
The delay model is retrained on verified historical outcomes, replacing the synthetic dataset this prototype ships with.
5. Screening and alerts
Scores refresh, thresholds fire, and alerts route to the responsible officer.
