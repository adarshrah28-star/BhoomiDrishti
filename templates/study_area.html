Why Uttarakhand

pick a district to load its profile

Most of the state sits on steep Himalayan slopes in seismic zones IV and V, with narrow valley floors, monsoon cloudbursts and long stretches of single all-weather access. Land is fragmented into small private holdings, so an alignment change of a few hundred metres can alter both the number of affected families and the slope stability picture. That combination is exactly what a pre-construction screening tool has to weigh.
